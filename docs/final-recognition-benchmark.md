| S2 — Good lighting, 2 hands | Bright office | Both | 720p webcam | 50cm |
| S3 — Dim lighting, 1 hand | Dim room | Single | 720p webcam | 50cm |
| S4 — Dim lighting, 2 hands | Dim room | Both | 720p webcam | 50cm |
| S5 — Laptop camera, 1 hand | Bright office | Single | Built-in | 50cm |
| S6 — Laptop camera, 2 hands | Bright office | Both | Built-in | 50cm |

## Methodology

- 100 recognition attempts total
- ~16–17 attempts per scenario
- 8 alphabet letters + 8 phrase gestures per scenario (randomly selected)
- Each attempt: hold gesture until prediction stabilizes, record result
- Measure: accuracy, confidence, FPS, latency
